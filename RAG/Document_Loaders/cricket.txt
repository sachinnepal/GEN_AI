The Game of Cricket

The bat is ready, the field is green,
The brightest game I've ever seen.
The bowler runs and sends the ball,
The batter swings, we cheer and call.

A cover drive flies through the air,
The crowd is shouting everywhere.
A wicket falls, the players cheer,
The final over now is near.

With every run and every fight,
Cricket fills our hearts with light.
Win or lose, we play with pride,
For cricket keeps our dreams alive.
